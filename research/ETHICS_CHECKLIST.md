# Human-Subject / Ethics Reporting Checklist

This project contains ratings provided by nine human annotators. Do not submit
an ethics statement until the facts below have been verified by the research
team.

## Facts currently supported by the project files

- Nine annotators rated 300 model-generated responses on a 0–100 scale.
- The annotation interface was browser-local: it states that ratings stayed
  in the browser until the annotator downloaded the ratings file.
- Annotators were not shown the decoding configuration or automated judge
  score according to the manuscript.
- The project files do NOT establish whether participants gave informed
  consent, whether they were compensated, whether an IRB/ethics committee
  review was required/obtained, or whether demographic data were collected.

## Before submission, verify and fill

1. Who were the annotators (e.g., students, colleagues, crowdworkers)?
2. Were they adults?
3. Was participation voluntary?
4. Was informed consent obtained?
5. Were annotators compensated? If yes, how?
6. Was any personally identifying information collected?
7. Was an IRB/ethics approval or exemption required by the institution?
8. If applicable, record the approval/exemption identifier.
9. Were participants told how the ratings would be used and released?
10. Confirm that the public reproducibility package contains anonymized IDs
    rather than participant names.

## Do not fabricate

Do not claim IRB approval, exemption, consent, compensation status, or
participant demographics unless those facts are verified by the authors.
If institutional policy determined that formal review was not required, state
that only after confirming the policy and wording with the institution.

## Suggested structure for the paper

"Human annotators rated model-generated responses independently using a
0–100 quality scale. The annotation procedure was [verified description].
[Verified consent/ethics statement]. No [verified sensitive/identifying data]
were included in the released reproducibility package."

Replace every bracketed statement with verified facts before submission.
